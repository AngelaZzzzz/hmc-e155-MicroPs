About

I’m a junior Electrical Engineering major at Harvey Mudd College. I’m happiest working where hardware meets software: writing RTL, bringing up boards, and chasing down bugs on an oscilloscope. I’ve built a multicycle RISC-V processor, a sensor-laden ocean robot, and two high-power rockets (I’m L2 certified). Outside of the lab, I spend my time with my dog Charlie, my hens Becky and Becca, and my 42 (still unnamed) quails.
